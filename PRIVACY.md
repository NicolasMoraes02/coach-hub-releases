# Coach Hub Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

## Who we are

Coach Hub is a team management app for high school soccer coaches and school athletic staff. This policy explains what information Coach Hub collects, how it is used, and the choices schools, coaches, players, and parents have.

Questions or requests: coach.hub.soccer@gmail.com

## Who uses Coach Hub

Coach Hub accounts are for coaches, school staff, and administrators. Students do not create accounts or sign in. Information about players is entered by their coaches on behalf of the school, and the school decides what is recorded.

Coach Hub is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used to record information about children under 13.

## Information we collect

Account information: email address, name, school and team names, role (owner, admin, coach, or viewer), and school logos and colors. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to Coach Hub.

Information coaches enter about players: name, jersey number, optional school student ID number, position, grade level, team, captain status, availability, and injury status with an optional expected return date and note.

Team activity: practice and game schedules and locations, attendance with optional notes or reasons, game lineups, goals, assists, cards, substitutions, minutes played, game notes, and private coach notes.

Expenses: expense reports and receipt photos. The vendor name and total are read from the receipt on the device itself; receipt photos are uploaded to private storage.

Player photos, when a coach adds them, are reduced to small square images and stored with the school's data so every device of the team can show them.

Technical information: when the app runs into a problem it records an error report with the error message, the screen, app version, operating system, device model, time, and the account and team involved. The app also keeps a random device identifier to synchronize changes between devices.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, device location, or browsing activity, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

## How we use information

To provide the app: storing team data, keeping phones and tablets in sync for the members of a school, producing reports and exports the coach asks for, and restoring backups.

To keep accounts secure, including two-step verification for platform administrators and a log of administrator support actions.

To find and fix problems using error reports.

We use player information only to provide Coach Hub to the school. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or build profiles of students.

## Where information is stored

On the device: team data is stored in encrypted storage on the phone or tablet so the app works offline.

In the cloud: when a coach signs in, team data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in the United States (Oregon). Information travels over encrypted connections. Database rules limit each school's data to that school's members.

Receipt photos and player photos are kept in private storage and opened through links that expire after a short time. Each device keeps a copy of the player photos so the app works offline, and that copy is erased when the coach signs out. School and team logos are stored so that anyone with the logo link can view them, because they appear on shared reports and images.

## Services that receive information

Supabase hosts accounts, the database, and file storage.

When a coach asks for game weather, the stadium address the coach typed is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder, and the resulting map coordinates are sent to the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). No player information is sent.

When the app checks for or downloads an update, it contacts GitHub, which receives standard connection information such as the device's IP address.

Text recognition for receipts and for imported schedules (PDF pages and photos) uses Google ML Kit running on the device; these images are not sent to Google for recognition.

Schedule files a coach imports (PDF, spreadsheet, document, calendar file, photo, or pasted text) are read on the device and are not uploaded. Only the games, practices, and events the coach chooses to add are saved with the team data.

When a coach exports or shares a PDF, CSV file, roster, or result image, it goes only to the apps and people the coach chooses.

The daily absence report (student names, student ID numbers, team, and absence reason) is sent from the coach's own email app to the school office address the coach enters. Coach Hub does not send it through its own servers.

## Who can see a school's information

Members of a school see that school's data according to their role. Viewers can read but not change information.

Coach Hub platform administrators can open a school's workspace to provide support. Administrator access requires two-step verification, and administrator actions that change accounts or schools are recorded.

We may disclose information if required by law, and only to the extent required.

## How long information is kept

Information is kept while the school or account uses Coach Hub. Closed seasons stay archived inside the school's workspace and are deleted when the school is deleted.

Error reports are kept only as long as needed to fix problems and are deleted with the account.

After deletion, copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a limited time until those backups expire.

## Deleting information

Delete your account in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. The app shows exactly what will be removed before anything is deleted.

Deleting your account removes your login and personal records right away. School data is not deleted with an account: if the school has other members it stays available to them, and if you were its only member a request to delete the school is sent to Coach Hub for review. The only owner of a school with other members must first make another member an owner.

Deleting a school: a school owner can request it in Settings, Danger zone. A Coach Hub administrator reviews every request, and only after approval are all of the school's teams, players, player photos, games, attendance, notes, expenses, receipt photos, and logos permanently deleted. The owner can cancel while the request is waiting for review.

Deleting the account also erases all Coach Hub data on that device. Coaches using Coach Hub without an account can erase their data in Settings, Danger zone.

Coaches can archive players and delete games, schedule events, attendance records, and notes at any time.

Students and parents who want information about a player corrected or deleted can contact the player's school or coach, or write to coach.hub.soccer@gmail.com. We will work with the school to respond.

## Security

Coach Hub uses encrypted connections, encrypted storage on the device, access rules for each school, private storage for receipts, and two-step verification for platform administrators. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a security incident affecting a school's information, we will notify the school.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the effective date below is updated and the new version is published at the same link and inside the app. Significant changes will be communicated to school owners.
